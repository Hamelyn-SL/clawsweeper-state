## Summary

El problema sigue presente en `dev`: `RootLayoutNav` consume Auth0 antes de renderizar `HamelynAuth0Provider`, así que los effects que deberían poblar `user_id` y `user_properties` siguen fuera del provider real.

## Plan

Fix https://github.com/Hamelyn-SL/hamelyn-serverless/issues/6488 in Supply. Current `apps/supply/app/_layout.tsx` calls `useAuth()` inside `RootLayoutNav` before `HamelynAuth0Provider` is rendered as a child, so the analytics identity and token sync effects never see the real Auth0 context. Make `RootLayoutNav` a descendant of `HamelynAuth0Provider`, or extract the identity/token sync into a child component mounted under the provider. Preserve the current intended `user.sub` GA4 `user_id` semantics unless a maintainer separately requests a Mongo `_id` migration. Do not edit generated `apps/supply/ios` or `apps/supply/android`. Keep the diff focused, add focused regression coverage if practical, and document that GA4 DebugView plus a BigQuery query must verify `user_id` and `app_language`, `app_version`, `platform`, `auth_state` after login.
