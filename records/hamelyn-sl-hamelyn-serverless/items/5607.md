Options:

- **Bounded retry plus visible error (recommended):** Respect `Retry-After` when present, keep a bounded retry budget, and persist a retriable label error on the orderSell when the budget is exhausted.
- **Indefinite deferred retry:** Keep labels pending and keep deferring 429s until MercadoLibre allows the download, accepting longer queue occupancy to avoid manual recovery.
- **Operational-only tuning:** Leave code as-is and handle remaining 429s through provider quota or deployment/runtime tuning outside this issue.
