## Summary

Keep open: current dev still ingests Wikipedia extracts and Commons/Wikipedia-derived author images, stores no license/attribution metadata, and renders the content on public author pages and JSON-LD without attribution.

## What This Changes

The issue asks Hamelyn to stop publishing unattributed Wikipedia text and Wikimedia Commons images on public author pages, either by avoiding licensed content or by adding attribution.

## Best Possible Solution

Choose Option A unless legal/product explicitly wants attribution: generate author descriptions only from CC0 structured facts, only re-host public-domain or CC0 Commons images, and add guards/tests so licensed text or media cannot reach the storefront without metadata.
