## What This Changes

The issue asks for `/api/amazon-notifications-pull` to stop before Vercel's 150s limit while preserving deletion of already processed SQS notifications and deferring the rest.

## Best Possible Solution

Add a current-dev per-message budget guard that defers unstarted SQS messages with an observable `execution_budget` reason, deletes completed messages before exit, and leaves unrelated Amazon retry or DB-liveness behavior unchanged.
